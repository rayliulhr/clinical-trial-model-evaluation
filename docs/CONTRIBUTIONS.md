# Contributions and authorship

Hongrui Liu is a Biomedical Sciences PhD candidate at Johns Hopkins University. His role in this collaborative project is scientific problem formulation, evaluation design and interpretation across clinical and computational work.

| Contribution | What Hongrui directed or specified |
|---|---|
| Clinical data quality | Trace candidate endpoint mappings, timing assumptions and eligibility rules to the source documentation. Preserve the prior version and flag decisions needing clinical adjudication. |
| Patient-model evaluation | Define the prediction time, who is eligible, which lab results could be used, and which simple risk models a longitudinal model must beat. Retain missing-lab patients and inspect their results separately. |
| Model comparison | Group related trials in evaluation splits, compare models on matched information, inspect saved predictions within families and investigate the weakest fold. |
| Trial design | Compare adjusted and unadjusted estimates using exactly the same reduced control groups, and specify the evidence needed before trying the approach on an independent trial. |

Clinical data preparation, original model architecture, statistical input and interpretation are collaborative. Hongrui directed the use of AI tools for source inspection, implementation of evaluation checks, and drafting. The public examples were written with AI assistance for this portfolio. Each has executable tests and deliberately fictional inputs.
